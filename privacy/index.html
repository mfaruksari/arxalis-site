ARXALIS Privacy Policy Effective date: September 29, 2026 Arxalis is designed as a local-first password and secure information manager. Your vault is stored encrypted on your device, and Arxalis does not operate a cloud service that receives or stores your vault contents. Information stored in Arxalis Arxalis can store passwords, login information, secure notes, identity information, payment card information, Wi-Fi credentials, tags, and two-factor authentication (TOTP) secrets. This vault data is stored locally on your device in encrypted form. No account, advertising, or tracking Arxalis does not require an Arxalis account. We do not use advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your personal data. Face ID and biometric authentication If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is performed by iOS using Apple system frameworks. Arxalis does not receive, read, or store your Face ID or other biometric templates. Camera access Camera access is used only when you choose to scan a QR code, such as when adding a two-factor authentication account. Camera content is processed for the requested scan and is not uploaded to an Arxalis server. Breached-password checks When you use the breached-password check, Arxalis hashes the password locally on your device. Only a short prefix of the resulting SHA-1 hash is sent to the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords service using its k-anonymity protocol. Your complete password and complete password hash are not sent to that service. Website icons (favicons) Arxalis may connect to websites associated with URLs you save in order to retrieve website icons. These requests may contact the saved website, its parent domain, or an icon/CDN resource referenced by the website. As with ordinary internet requests, the remote service may receive standard network information such as your IP address. Arxalis does not send your vault password or stored account password as part of favicon requests. AutoFill Arxalis supports iOS AutoFill through its credential provider extension. Information needed for AutoFill is handled using Apple-provided app and Keychain mechanisms on the device. Arxalis does not provide a remote server for storing your AutoFill credentials. Backups, imports, exports, and sharing Arxalis can create encrypted backups and can import or export supported data. These files are created under your control. If you choose to save or share a file through iCloud Drive, another cloud provider, email, messaging, or another service, that service's privacy practices apply to the copy you choose to send or store there. Third-party services Certain optional features may communicate with third-party internet services, including Have I Been Pwned for breached-password checks and websites/CDNs for favicon retrieval. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Children's privacy Arxalis is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Arxalis does not operate a user account system or cloud database for vault contents. Changes to this policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when Arxalis features or data practices change.
